# Virtual Campus of Gachon University

A team-built Roblox recreation of Gachon University's campus for the 2022 Metaverse Contest.

## Purpose

Our team wanted to introduce the AI Engineering Building and the surrounding campus to students who rarely visited that part of the university. We chose a spring setting, including the cherry blossoms that made returning to campus memorable.

## Project

Built in Roblox Studio, the campus includes the AI Engineering Building, sports field, dormitories, roads, and surrounding scenery. Visitors can explore with vehicles and other interactive objects.

The layout connects familiar campus landmarks so visitors can understand how the spaces relate to one another.
